# Authentication

Use the master tokens. Keep the brand, title and explanation outside the form
card. Phone gutters are 16–24dp; form width is capped at 480dp. At 960dp with
normal text, use two columns capped at 1040dp. Large text stays single-column.
Preserve autofill, password paste, inline errors and existing authentication
actions. Decorative background shapes are replaced by a calm neutral surface.
